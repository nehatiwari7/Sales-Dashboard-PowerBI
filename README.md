# 📊 Sales Dashboard — Power BI

An interactive retail sales dashboard built in Power BI, designed to give a clear, drill-down view of sales performance across products, categories, payment modes, and time periods.

---

## 📌 Overview

Retail businesses generate large volumes of transactional data, but raw numbers alone don't drive decisions — visibility does. This dashboard turns **3.64 lakh (364,000) retail transactions** into an interactive tool for tracking sales, profit, and payment trends at a glance, with the ability to drill down into any specific product, category, or time period.

---

## 📈 Key Metrics

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | ₹364.15K |
| Total Profit | ₹32K |
| Profit Margin | 9.52% |

---

## ✨ Features

- **Sales & Profit Trend by Month** — track performance over time and spot seasonal patterns
- **Payment Mode Split** — compare Online vs. Cash transactions
- **Sales by Product, Category & Sale Type** — identify top-performing products and categories
- **Daily Sales Volatility Analysis** — highlight unusually high/low sales days
- **Interactive Filters** — Year filter (2024 / 2025) and Month slicer for flexible time-based analysis

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques

- **Power BI Desktop** — dashboard design and visualization
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** — custom measures for Profit, Sales, and Payment Mode KPIs
- **Data Modeling** — structured relationships between tables for accurate, fast drill-downs

---

## ✅ Conclusion

This dashboard demonstrates how raw retail transaction data can be transformed into an actionable, decision-ready tool. By combining DAX-driven KPIs with interactive slicers and drill-downs, stakeholders can independently explore sales trends, profit drivers, and payment behavior without needing a new report for every question.
